How to read a district number

Every district number has three digits. The first two digits tell you whose station area it is. The last digit tells you what is in that area.

First two digits: station area

First two digitsStation area
60xGlenside station (Glenside Fire Protection District)
61xStation 61 (Glen Ellyn HQ, downtown)
62xStation 62 (Glen Ellyn, south of Roosevelt Rd)

Last digit: what is in the area

Last digitMeaningExamples on the map
0No hydrants600, 610, 620
3Hydrants603, 613, 623
5Newer area close to the train tracks; also used to help with mutual aid ambulances605, 615, 625
6Target hazard606, 616, 626
7Immediate care (Duly / Northwestern Medicine)617, 627
8Multifamily618, 628
Putting it together: 615 = Station 61's area, newer area near the train. 626 = Station 62's area, target hazard. 603 = Glenside station's area, hydrants.

Still to confirm with your officer:

  • What "help with mutual aid ambulances" means for 5 districts. It was asked in the Jan 30, 2024 thread, but the screenshot shows no answer.
  • Which Glenside station covers the 60x districts, and its address. (Glenside FPD headquarters is at 1608 Bloomingdale Rd, Glendale Heights.)
  • Which buildings are in the 618 and 628 multifamily pockets.

Map legend (hydrant dots): greater than 1,500 GPM · 1,000–1,499 GPM · 500–999 GPM · less than 500 GPM · no data / not a Glen Ellyn water system hydrant. The black outline is the municipal (village) boundary.
